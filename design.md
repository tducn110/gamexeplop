# Design System

## Direction

- Game-first interface
- Warm paper, bamboo, and harvest palette
- Be Vietnam Pro throughout
- No landing-page hero patterns
- Pixi stage is the primary surface, overlays stay compact and operational

## UI Rules

- Buttons use pill shapes and warm CTA contrast.
- Panels use rounded cards with warm paper fills and light inset highlights.
- HUD stays dense and practical.
- Dashboard and settings are overlays, not separate marketing screens.
- Text should stay compact and readable on mobile.
